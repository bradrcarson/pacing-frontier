Pacing Frontier AI

This document catalogs the proposals to pause, pace or contain frontier AI, with pros, cons and open questions for each. Further tabs cover verification, China, the economy, ownership, law and history.

Map of the Proposals

Placement follows the comparison matrix. Sixteen of the main entries need no agreement with China, and thirteen of those keep development going or slow it. Six of the nine entries that stop development need China.

Purpose and Scope

This is a working document, built to be extended by research agents. Each tab ends with open questions they can take up.

  • What it covers. Every substantial proposal to pause, pace, gate or contain frontier AI development, from 2023 through October 2026.
  • What each entry gives. The proposal in its proponents’ terms, how it works, pros, cons, economic effects, what it means for ownership of the AI companies, and a list of open questions.
  • What the cross-cutting tabs give. The issues every proposal runs into: verification, China, the economy, ownership, law, politics and history.
  • What it leaves out. The document does not rank the proposals or recommend one. Rules aimed only at near-term harms, such as bias or privacy, are outside its scope.
  • Where Brad Carson’s unpublished proposals sit. The Two-Lane Plan, Embargo Plus U.S. Pause, the tripwire bill, the containment rule for agentic training and the liability regime each have an entry beside the published proposals. The Two-Lane Plan is developed in full in Two-Lane Plan: Full Design. The first write-ups of the two plans are in The Two-Lane Plan for Frontier AI.

How the Document Is Organized

The proposals are sorted into four families by what they do to frontier development. Nine further tabs cover the issues that every proposal runs into, and one tab develops the Two-Lane Plan in full.

The Four Families

  • StopHalts or prohibits frontier developmentStop: Pauses and Bans
  • PaceSlows it through throttles, triggers and gatesPace: Slowdowns and Gates
  • Contain and ChannelKeeps it going under state control or toward safer systemsContain and Channel
  • ContinueRejects pausing and pacingThe Case Against Pausing

The Two-Lane Plan also has a tab of its own, Two-Lane Plan: Full Design, which develops it into a statute, institutions, operating rules and financing.
